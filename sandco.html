SandCo.

A long-trusted industrial supplier, relaunched as a modern brand.

Client
SandCo
Sector
Industrial · quartz sand production
Scope
Strategy · Identity · Website · Culture
01 · Context

A strong business, with a brand from a different era.

SandCo is a quartz sand producer supplying high-grade material to the oil and gas, uranium and adjacent industries. The product was excellent, the customer base was stable, and the company was profitable.

But the brand had not kept pace with the business. Its identity, its online presence, and its internal culture all reflected a company from a different era of industry. That gap had begun to cost SandCo credibility with the buyers and partners shaping the next decade of the sector.

02 · What we did

A full rebrand — and a shift in how the company thinks.

We delivered a full rebrand and relaunch: strategic positioning, a new identity, a new website, and a refreshed presence across every customer touchpoint.

The work went deeper than the surface. We worked with the C-suite to shift how leadership thought about the brand, and with operations to align the internal culture and language with the external story. The relaunch became the lever for a broader change inside the business — including a clearer case for reinvestment into modern machinery and modern processes.

03 · Outcome

On the radar of markets it had been invisible to.

SandCo relaunched with a modern brand and a strategic position calibrated for the next decade of industrial buyers. The relaunch was covered by mining.com and Yahoo Finance, putting the company on the radar of markets it had previously been invisible to.

Inside the company, the rebrand reset how the leadership team talks about its own work.
